关于我About Me

Hi! 我是 bohui，目前在 香港中文大学（深圳） 攻读硕士学位，主要关注 AI Infra / Serverless / Agent 相关方向。我所在的实验室是 S4 Lab（Scalable, Sustainable, and Stable Systems Lab），师从 余旻晨教授。

我的研究兴趣最初起源于 Serverless Computing，主要关注如何在云原生环境下实现更高效、更弹性、也更低成本的计算资源管理。随着 LLM serving 的快速发展，我的研究方向也逐渐延伸到 AI Infrastructure，尤其关注大模型推理系统中的调度、弹性扩缩容、KVCache 管理以及长上下文推理等问题。

此外，我一直对新技术和新范式保持较高的兴趣与开放度。随着 Agent、Agent Infra 与 Agentic System 逐渐成为重要的研究方向，我也开始关注如何为更复杂、更动态的智能体应用构建稳定、高效、可扩展的系统支撑。对我来说，做这些工作的核心动机，是希望理解并参与构建下一代 AI 应用背后的基础设施。

Hi! I'm Bohui, currently a master's student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, working on AI Infra / Serverless / Agent. I'm part of S4 Lab (Scalable, Sustainable, and Stable Systems Lab), advised by Prof. Minchen Yu.

My research interest started from serverless computing — how to manage cloud-native compute more efficiently, elastically, and cost-effectively. As LLM serving evolved rapidly, my focus naturally extended to AI infrastructure, especially scheduling, elastic scaling, KV-cache management, and long-context inference in large-model serving systems.

I also try to stay open to new paradigms. As agents, agent infrastructure, and agentic systems are becoming an important research direction, I've been thinking about how to build stable, efficient, and scalable system support for these increasingly complex and dynamic workloads. The driving motivation, for me, is to understand and help build the infrastructure behind the next generation of AI applications.
